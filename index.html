FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to clear up booking requirements, delivery services, and policies.

To hire a vehicle, you will need a valid driver's license held for at least 1 year, a passport or valid national identity card, and a major credit card in the primary driver's name. International drivers may require an International Driving Permit (IDP) if their domestic license is not in English/Latin characters.

Yes. A temporary pre-authorization hold is placed on your credit card at the time of delivery. The amount ranges from $500 to $2,500 depending on the vehicle class (e.g., higher for sports cars). The hold is fully released within 24-48 hours after returning the car in its original condition.

Absolutely. We offer doorstep delivery and pickup as standard services. You can select delivery to your home, office, hotel, or directly to airport VIP arrival lounges. Free delivery is included for rentals of 3 days or more within our standard operating zones.

The minimum rental age is 21 years. Drivers aged 21-24 are allowed to book sports, electric, and standard SUVs, but are subject to a young driver premium ($30/day) and an increased security deposit hold. Luxury and supercar categories (e.g., S-Class, Porsche 911) require the driver to be at least 25 years old.

We provide free cancellations with a full 100% refund up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled rental start time. Cancellations made within less than 24 hours will incur a one-day rental charge penalty, and the remainder of your booking payment will be refunded.
